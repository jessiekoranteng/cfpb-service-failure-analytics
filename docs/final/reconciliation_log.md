outputs in this repository. Each row is a place where two artefacts disagreed, or where an earlier artefact promised
something that was not delivered. "Governing source" is the artefact the Final Project treats as correct; where an earlier
document was wrong it is **not rewritten** (those modules are submitted) and is superseded by this log.
